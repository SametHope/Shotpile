polish: English UI, filmstrip, coloured swipe feedback, smoother motion

- The whole UI is now English only (app.js, index.html, smoke assertions).
- Clicking a card opens the photo viewer: the gesture captures the pointer, so
  the click lands on the card, not the image. A `dragged` flag keeps a swipe
  from triggering it.
- The queue filmstrip shows the previous few decisions and the next few
  photos; clicking an item jumps back to it.
- The active stamp fills with colour as you drag (red/green/yellow), and the
  card animates off-screen instead of vanishing. The exit animation holds the
  decision until the card has gone, so a swipe never flashes the finale.
- The footer bar expands/collapses with a transition, so showing it no longer
  shifts the content.
- Scrollbars are hidden globally and the view stops clipping during a review,
  so a swipe no longer throws scrollbars or clips the card.
- The stage keeps clearance below it so the deck cards never overlap the
  Delete/Skip/Keep buttons.
- The image area uses a soft solid wash instead of a hard checkerboard, so a
  small image no longer sits in a huge transparent-pattern area.
- Month statistics carry SVG icons and per-stat accent colours.
